I am Vlad Maftei, a 3D generalist from Bucharest working across VFX, CGI, product imagery, digital humans, procedural motion, and AI-assisted image and video.

My work moves between commercial product renders, animation tests, digital human studies, abstract simulations, environment pieces, and AI-driven film experiments. I like projects where image-making, lookdev, motion, and technical problem solving meet, and where generative tools are steered by real 3D structure instead of luck.
